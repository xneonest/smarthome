No keys. No worries.

A smart lock is the easiest first step into home automation. Family members unlock with face or fingerprint, domestic staff get their own card or passcode, and guests receive a one-time password that expires after use. Every entry is logged in the app, and you get an alert when the door opens.

Frequently asked questions

Will a smart lock fit my existing door?

Most wooden and many metal main doors are compatible. We check the door thickness and cut-out during the site visit and recommend the right model.

What happens if the battery runs out?

You get low-battery warnings well in advance, and every model has an emergency option such as manual keys or USB power.

Can I give temporary access to guests or staff?

Yes. Create one-time passwords for guests and individual cards or codes for staff, and see every entry in the app.

Do you install locks outside Mumbai city?

Yes, we install across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane.
