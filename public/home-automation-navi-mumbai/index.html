What we automate

Lighting & scenes

Dimming, colour temperature and one-tap scenes like Good Morning, Movie Night and Good Night. Lights follow your routine instead of you walking room to room.

Smart door locks

3D face recognition, fingerprint, RFID card, passcode and one-time passwords for guests and staff — with a log of who entered and when.

Touch panels & switches

Glass touch switches and 3.5", 4" and 6" wall panels that control lights, fans, ACs, curtains and music from one place.

Curtains & blinds

Silent curtain motors with precise positioning, scheduled to open at sunrise or close automatically for privacy.

AC, TV & appliances

An IR blaster lets you control existing ACs, TVs and music systems from your phone or by voice — no new appliances needed.

Security & monitoring

Smart video door phones, motion sensors and CCTV integration with real-time alerts, so you can see who is at the door from anywhere.

Everything works with Alexa, Google Home, Apple Home, Siri and SmartThings, and runs on Zigbee, Wi-Fi, Matter, Thread and Bluetooth for fast, reliable control.

Frequently asked questions

Is it better to automate at possession or after moving in?

At possession or during interiors is ideal because wired panels and curtain motors can be planned in. But we also retrofit homes that are already furnished, using wireless devices.

Do you install smart locks in Navi Mumbai?

Yes. We supply and install smart door locks with face recognition, fingerprint, RFID card, passcode and OTP access across Navi Mumbai.

How long does installation take?

A smart lock or single room usually takes a day. A full-home system depends on size and whether it is wired or wireless; we share a timeline with the quote.

Can I control my home when I am away?

Yes. The Neonest app lets you control devices, check the video door phone and receive security alerts from anywhere with internet.
