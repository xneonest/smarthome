Home automation built for Mumbai apartments

Most Mumbai homes are apartments with finished interiors, concealed wiring and limited space for new equipment. Neonest is designed for exactly that: compact Zigbee gateways, touch switches that fit standard modular boxes, and retrofit controllers that sit behind your existing switches. For homes under construction or interior renovation, we plan wired automation with your architect or electrician from the start.

Whether you want to switch off every light from bed, let the maid in with a one-time password while you are at work, or have the AC cool the bedroom before you reach home, we build the system around how your family lives.

What we automate

Lighting & scenes

Dimming, colour temperature and one-tap scenes like Good Morning, Movie Night and Good Night. Lights follow your routine instead of you walking room to room.

Smart door locks

3D face recognition, fingerprint, RFID card, passcode and one-time passwords for guests and staff — with a log of who entered and when.

Touch panels & switches

Glass touch switches and 3.5", 4" and 6" wall panels that control lights, fans, ACs, curtains and music from one place.

Curtains & blinds

Silent curtain motors with precise positioning, scheduled to open at sunrise or close automatically for privacy.

AC, TV & appliances

An IR blaster lets you control existing ACs, TVs and music systems from your phone or by voice — no new appliances needed.

Security & monitoring

Smart video door phones, motion sensors and CCTV integration with real-time alerts, so you can see who is at the door from anywhere.

Everything works with Alexa, Google Home, Apple Home, Siri and SmartThings, and runs on Zigbee, Wi-Fi, Matter, Thread and Bluetooth for fast, reliable control.

Frequently asked questions

How much does home automation cost in Mumbai?

It depends on the number of rooms and devices. A single-room or smart-lock setup costs far less than a full-home system. Neonest offers Basic, Elite and Premium packages, and we give a device-by-device quote after a free site visit.

Can you automate my flat without breaking walls?

Yes. Our retrofit controllers and wireless devices work with your existing wiring, so most finished Mumbai apartments can be automated without chasing walls or repainting.

Will it work with Alexa and Google Home?

Yes. Neonest works with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, Apple Home, Siri and SmartThings, and supports Matter, Zigbee, Thread, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

Which areas of Mumbai do you serve?

We serve Mumbai city and suburbs, as well as Navi Mumbai and Thane. Call or WhatsApp us with your location to book a visit.
